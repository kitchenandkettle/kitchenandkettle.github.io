Farm Fresh Eggs

Don't wash clean eggs. Freshly laid eggs have a natural coating called the bloom — an invisible seal that keeps bacteria out. Unwashed eggs with the bloom intact stay fresh on the counter for 2–3 weeks.

If dirty, wash right. Use warm water (warmer than the egg — never cold). Rub gently, no soap. Dry immediately. Refrigerate washed eggs.

Store pointy-end-down. Unwashed → counter. Washed → fridge. Once refrigerated, don't move back.

Float test: Fill a bowl with cool water. Sinks flat = fresh. Stands up = still good, use soon. Floats = crack into a separate bowl and check.
