Farm Fresh Eggs

Handling

Wash before eating. Rinse eggs under warm water — warmer than the egg, never cold. No soap. Rub gently with your fingers. Dry with a clean cloth if returning to the carton.

Storage: Washed eggs last 2 months in the refrigerator. Unwashed eggs last 3 months refrigerated, or 2 weeks on the counter. Store pointy-end-down — it keeps the yolk centered and slows aging.

Float test: Sinks flat → fresh. Stands up → use soon. Floats → crack separately & check.
