## Description (copy-paste)
From the nest box to the kitchen.

A single-page printable reference card for handling farm-fresh eggs. Covers everything you need to know in one place: the bloom (and why you shouldn't wash clean eggs), when and how to wash properly, counter vs. fridge storage, the float test, and quick daily tips. Not the egg police — just practical information that works.

**What's on the card:**
- The bloom — what it is, why it matters, when to leave it alone
- Washing — when it's necessary, the right water temperature, and the do's and don'ts
- Storage — where to put unwashed vs. washed eggs (and why pointy-end-down matters)
- The float test — how to check freshness in seconds
- Quick tips — dating eggs, the separate-bowl rule, collecting routines

**Designed to print and keep.** Tack it on the fridge, pin it by the egg basket, or slip it into your kitchen binder. One page. All the essentials.
