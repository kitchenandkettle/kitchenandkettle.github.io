How to Use This Binder

This binder is divided into six tabbed sections, each covering a different part of running a household. You don't need to fill everything out at once — start with the section that's causing the most friction right now and build from there.

Home Information — Keep contacts, account numbers, and utility details in one place so you're not digging through email when the power goes out.

Cleaning System — Daily, weekly, monthly, and seasonal checklists. Check off as you go — the binder remembers what you've done.

Home Maintenance — Appliance logs, repair records, warranty dates, and paint colors. The stuff you swear you'll remember and never do.

Financial Organization — Bills, subscriptions, budget, and savings goals. No fancy finance software, just awareness.

Food & Kitchen — Pantry and freezer inventory, meal planning, and grocery lists so you stop buying what you already have.

Family Organization — Important dates, gift planning, holidays, and vacations. The calendar section that lives on paper.

Everything you type stays saved in your browser. Print it, fill it by hand, or do both — whatever works.
