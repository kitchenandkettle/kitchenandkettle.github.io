## Description (copy-paste)
A proper home for your recipe collection. Ten pre-made category dividers, two write-in custom tabs, and two recipe insert cards — everything you need to organize your recipes by what you actually cook.

Print on letter paper, cut the tabs, and slip into any binder. The cover page has a fillable name field. The recipe inserts give you a clean format to write down your favorites and file them behind the right divider. Type directly into the interactive version — your categories and recipes save to your device.

**What's inside (15 pages):**
- Fillable cover page with your name
- 10 pre-made category dividers: Breakfast, Soups & Stews, Salads & Sides, Main Dishes, Breads & Baking, Desserts, Preserves & Pickles, Drinks, Snacks & Appetizers, Holiday & Special Occasions
- 2 write-in custom category pages for whatever doesn't fit the pre-made list
- 2 recipe insert cards — clean template for writing down your own recipes and filing them behind the right divider
- Clay kettle logo on every divider page

**Designed to pair with the Recipe Cards** — same letter-size format, same warm paper and real typography. Print the dividers and inserts together, add the recipe cards, and you've got a complete recipe binder.
