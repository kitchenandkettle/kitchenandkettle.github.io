## Description (copy-paste)
A journal for tracking the full moons through the year.

The old moon names came from what was happening on the ground. The Worm Moon meant thawing soil. The Strawberry Moon meant it was time to pick. The Harvest Moon meant extra light for bringing in the crops. This journal walks through all twelve full moons — Wolf Moon through Cold Moon — with the folk meaning behind each one and a prompt to help you check in with yourself once a month.

Beyond the twelve full moons, each moon gets its own set of cycle pages — New Moon for intentions, Waxing Moon for action, Full Moon for reflection, Waning Moon for release. That's a full cycle of reflection for every moon of the year: 68 pages total. Plus seasonal moon notes that tie the moons to what's happening outside your window, and open notes pages for whatever doesn't fit a category.
