## Description (copy-paste)
A menu for your own kitchen, because deciding what to eat every night is enough work already.

Open the interactive file, choose a style, name your kitchen, and fill in your dishes. Post it where everyone can see it and point at the menu when somebody asks what's for dinner.

**Choose from three menu styles:**
- **The Full Spread** — Appetizers, Soups & Salads, Sides, Drinks, Mains, and Desserts in a two-column restaurant-style layout
- **The Simple Board** — Sides, Quick Bites, Mains, and Desserts for weeknight cooking
- **The Greatest Hits** — Three custom categories for the meals your household actually makes

**What you get:**
- An interactive HTML menu — type directly in your browser
- Three fillable menu styles in one file
- Automatic local saving on your device — no account, no cloud, fully private
- A printable Letter-size layout designed for standard home printer paper
- A PDF version for printing and saving to your files
- Blank placeholder examples that can be replaced with your own kitchen's meals

Type into the menu and save your entries locally, or print a blank copy and fill it out by hand. Switch styles whenever you want. Your kitchen, your rules.

▶ This is a digital download. No physical product will be shipped.
▶ To open: double-click the HTML file to open it in your browser. If that doesn't work, drag and drop the file into any open browser window (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). No special software needed.
▶ For a clean printout, uncheck "Headers and footers" in your browser's print dialog before printing or saving as PDF.
▶ Your entries save to your device automatically. Clearing your browser data will clear saved entries.
▶ To print: File → Print. Use letter-size paper (8.5×11"), 100% scale.
▶ To save as PDF: File → Print → Save as PDF (built into every browser).
▶ Works with GoodNotes, Notability, and any PDF app.
