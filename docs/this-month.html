July

Peak abundance

Everything ripens at once. The kitchen's a mess, the garden needs picking every day, and I wouldn't trade this month for anything.

What's in season now

Tomatoes that actually taste like tomatoes. Zucchini — so much zucchini. Blueberries by the handful. Basil that fills the kitchen when you walk past it. Green beans, cucumbers, raspberries. Garlic scapes are curling — bulbs in a few weeks.

What to put by

If you have hens, they're laying like crazy right now. You cannot eat this many eggs. Freeze them or lime them — you'll want them in January when the girls slow down.

Berries into jam. Cucumbers into pickles. Green beans into the freezer. You don't have to can everything — the freezer works fine for most of it.

Kitchen rhythm

The oven stays off. Grill extra vegetables, eat the rest tomorrow cold. One batch of pesto in the freezer now beats any meal plan — it tastes like July all winter.

If dinner is sliced tomatoes with salt and a piece of bread, that counts. You're feeding people. It's enough.

Around the house

Overcast day? Wash two windows and call it done. The whole house doesn't need doing at once — two at a time, whenever the clouds show up.

If your cleaning routine feels like something you're avoiding, it's too complicated. Strip it back to what actually needs doing.
