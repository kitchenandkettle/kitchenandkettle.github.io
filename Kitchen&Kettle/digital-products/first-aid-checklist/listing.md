## Description (copy-paste)
Practical first aid for the cuts, burns, bites, and mishaps of everyday life. Not a medical textbook — a real-kitchen reference designed to grab with one hand while the other is under cold water.

Clear step-by-step care for the injuries that actually happen at home: cuts and scrapes, burns, splinters, sprains, insect bites, kitchen injuries, nosebleeds, choking, eye injuries, ear issues, and poisoning. Each scenario includes when to handle it yourself and when to get to a doctor.

**What's inside (16 pages):**
- 11 injury scenarios with clear, numbered steps
- Folk remedies that actually work — duct tape for splinters, baking soda paste for stings, plantain poultices, and more
- 22-item first aid kit checklist with clickable checkboxes
- Fillable emergency contacts page — doctor, poison control, pharmacy, and more
- Household info section — allergies, medical conditions, home address

**Interactive features:** Check off steps as you go — your progress saves automatically to your device. Fill in emergency contacts and household info once, and they stay. Print a clean copy anytime.

**This is not** a first-aid certification manual. It won't teach you CPR or how to suture a wound. It's the stuff that happens in real kitchens and households — the cuts, the burns, the splinters — and a clear line for when you should put the checklist down and call 911.
