## Description (copy-paste)
Know what's normal. Record what changed.

The useful livestock health record is the one you can find when you need it. This printable and interactive record keeps animal profiles, baseline notes, health events, treatments, preventative care, weight checks, and vet visits together in one place.

It is designed for small farms and homesteads caring for goats, sheep, pigs, cattle, rabbits, poultry flocks, and other animals. Use one profile for an individual animal, or use the overview pages for a flock or group.

**What's inside (13 pages):**
- **How to use the record** — A simple system for recording observations, dates, products, instructions, and follow-up
- **Farm contacts** — Veterinarian, emergency clinic, feed supplier, animal transport, farm helper, address, and directions
- **Animal overview** — A whole-farm list for names, species, breeds, identification, dates, and locations
- **Individual animal profile** — Identification, markings, source, parentage, location, and important history
- **Baseline health** — Record what normal appetite, behavior, appearance, movement, and output look like
- **Health event record** — What changed, what you observed, who you contacted, what was done, and what happened next
- **Treatment log** — Product, reason, dose, route, next dose, end date, and withdrawal period
- **Preventative care log** — Vaccines, routine care, product and lot numbers, and next due dates
- **Weight and condition tracker** — Track measurements over time and note why you checked
- **Vet visit record** — Findings, exact instructions, prescriptions, follow-up, and documents stored
- **Emergency notes** — Feeding, medication, animal locations, decision-makers, and current instructions
- **Reusable writing space** — Forms are designed to print again when you need more copies

**Two formats included:**
- **Interactive HTML** — Type directly into the record. Entries save automatically to your device. Save a backup copy or import one later. No account or cloud storage needed.
- **Printable PDF** — Print blank copies and fill them in by hand. Print individual pages as needed or keep the complete record in a binder.

**Important note:** This is a recordkeeping tool, not a diagnosis guide or veterinary treatment manual. It does not tell you what disease an animal has or how to treat one. For urgent concerns, contact a veterinarian and follow the product label and your veterinarian's instructions.
