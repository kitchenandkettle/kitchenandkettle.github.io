Health Record

Know what's normal. Record what changed.

A practical record system for the animals in your care — profiles, baseline notes, health events, treatments, preventative care, and vet visits.

A recordkeeping tool, not a diagnosis guide.

How to use this record

02

The useful health record is the one you can find when you need it. Keep this with your farm paperwork, fill it out while the details are fresh, and bring the relevant pages to a vet visit.

What's inside

  1. Farm contacts — Keep your veterinarian, emergency contacts, feed supplier, and transport information together.
  2. Animal overview — Get the whole farm or flock on one page before you start individual records.
  3. Profiles and baselines — Record what is normal for each animal. That gives you something useful to compare when behavior changes.
  4. Health events and treatments — Write down what you saw, what you did, and what happened next.
  5. Preventative care, weight, and vet visits — Keep recurring care and professional advice from disappearing into scraps of paper.

Keep the record factual

Write “eating less and standing apart from the group” rather than guessing at a diagnosis. Exact observations, dates, product names, doses, and instructions are more useful than confident-sounding labels.

A note on safety

This product does not tell you what disease an animal has or how to treat one. For urgent concerns, contact a veterinarian. Follow the product label and your veterinarian's instructions for medications, vaccines, withdrawal periods, and handling.

If an animal is struggling to breathe, cannot stand, is bleeding heavily, is severely bloated, or has another emergency sign, call a veterinarian rather than spending time filling out the form.
