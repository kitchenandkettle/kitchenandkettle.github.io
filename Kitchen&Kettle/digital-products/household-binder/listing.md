## Description (copy-paste)
A central place to manage your home — no apps, no scattered notes, no passwords on sticky notes. If you've ever stood in the cleaning aisle wondering what you actually need, or stared at a pile of bills with no system, or tried to remember when you last changed the furnace filter — this takes the guesswork out of all of it.

This is the binder you wish came with the house. Fill it out once, keep it on a shelf, and stop digging through email every time the power goes out or you need the plumber's number.

**What's inside (38 pages):**

Section 1 — Home Information: household details, emergency contacts, important phone numbers, utility accounts, password hints, and pet information (vet, microchip, feeding, meds).

Section 2 — Cleaning System: daily, weekly, monthly, and seasonal deep-cleaning checklists with clickable checkboxes. Room-by-room cleaning tracker. Fill-in-your-own sections for every checklist.

Section 3 — Home Maintenance: seasonal maintenance checklist (spring through winter, including septic and boiler tasks), appliance maintenance log, home repair log (25 rows), warranty tracker, paint color records.

Section 4 — Financial Organization: bill tracker with paid checkboxes, subscription tracker, household budget worksheet, savings goals.

Section 5 — Food & Kitchen: pantry inventory, freezer inventory, weekly meal planner, grocery list organized by store section, holiday meal planner.

Section 6 — Family Organization: important dates, gift planning tracker, holiday planning, vacation planning.

Plus: a general notes page at the end for anything that didn't fit in the sections above.

**Interactive features:** Everything you type saves automatically to your device — checkboxes, text fields, notes, all of it. Toolbar buttons let you save a backup file, import a backup, or clear and start fresh. Print a clean copy anytime.

**This is not** a financial planner, a diet tracker, or a substitute for a password manager. It's a paper-based system for the household information that doesn't belong in an app — the stuff you need when the internet is down, when you're on the phone with the insurance company, or when someone else needs to run your house for a few days.
