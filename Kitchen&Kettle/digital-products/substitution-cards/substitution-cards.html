Ingredient Substitutions

Quick reference for when you're mid-recipe and out of something

Dairy

4 substitutions
  • Buttermilk→1 cup milk + 1 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar (let sit 5 min)
  • Heavy cream→⅓ cup melted butter + ¾ cup whole milk
  • Sour cream→1 cup plain yogurt (full-fat works best)
  • Cream cheese→1 cup ricotta, or blend cottage cheese until smooth

Eggs

4 substitutions
  • 1 egg (baking)→¼ cup unsweetened applesauce
  • 1 egg (binding)→1 tbsp flaxseed meal + 3 tbsp water (let sit 5 min)
  • 1 egg (moisture)→½ mashed ripe banana
  • Egg wash→Milk, cream, or melted butter brushed on

Baking

5 substitutions
  • Baking powder→¼ tsp baking soda + ½ tsp cream of tartar (per 1 tsp)
  • Baking soda→3 tsp baking powder (per 1 tsp) — results may be less crisp
  • Self-rising flour→1 cup AP flour + 1½ tsp baking powder + ¼ tsp salt
  • Cornstarch→2 tbsp AP flour (per 1 tbsp)
  • Cake flour→1 cup AP flour minus 2 tbsp + 2 tbsp cornstarch

Ingredient Substitutions

Quick reference for when you're mid-recipe and out of something

Sweeteners

4 substitutions
  • Brown sugar→1 cup white sugar + 1 tbsp molasses (light) or 2 tbsp (dark)
  • Honey→1¼ cup sugar + ¼ cup liquid from the recipe (per 1 cup)
  • Powdered sugar→Blend 1 cup granulated sugar until fine (not for frosting)
  • Maple syrup→1 cup honey, or 1 cup sugar + ¼ cup liquid (per 1 cup)

Fats

4 substitutions
  • Butter (baking)→1 cup shortening, or ¾ cup neutral oil (per 1 cup)
  • Butter (general)→1 cup margarine or coconut oil (per 1 cup)
  • Oil (baking)→1 cup applesauce, or 1 cup melted butter (per 1 cup)
  • Shortening→1 cup butter + 2 tbsp (per 1 cup) — adds water content

Pantry

5 substitutions
  • Breadcrumbs→Crushed crackers, rolled oats, or crushed cornflakes
  • Lemon juice→½ tsp white vinegar (per 1 tsp)
  • Tomato paste→3 tbsp tomato sauce + reduce liquid elsewhere (per 1 tbsp)
  • Soy sauce→Tamari (GF) or coconut aminos (sweeter, less salt)
  • Worcestershire→Equal parts soy sauce + vinegar + pinch of brown sugar
