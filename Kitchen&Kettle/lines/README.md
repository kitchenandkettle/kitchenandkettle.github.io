# Kitchen & Kettle Lines

Kitchen & Kettle is the parent brand. Lines live here when they have a distinct editorial lens or product direction but do not need a separate brand, website, or shop.

## Current lines

- `wise-woman/` — research-led old-world knowledge translated for modern life

## Boundary

A line may have its own working documents, experiments, products, and visual notes. Shared Kitchen & Kettle infrastructure stays at the parent level:

- `site/` — website source
- `docs/` — GitHub Pages deployment copy
- `digital-products/` — product production files
- `shop-info/` — shared shop and brand materials
- `scripts/` — shared automation

Do not duplicate the website, PDF pipeline, or shop infrastructure inside a line unless there is a clear technical need.
