# Fall Mornings — Prototype Outline

## Status

First seasonal content prototype to investigate. Not a finished product or public launch.

Winter Mornings remains the next seasonal edition to develop after this test.

## Working question

What small old-world practices helped people prepare for the colder, darker part of the year, and what can still help a modern person move through fall without turning life into a seasonal project?

## Audience problem

The season is changing. Mornings are darker and cooler, work is still demanding, and the shift toward winter can feel like something to endure rather than prepare for.

## Editorial frame

Fall is a threshold season: gather, preserve, warm, and notice the return of darkness.

Historical evidence will be labeled rather than smoothed into one imaginary ancient tradition. The modern version must work for someone with limited time, limited space, and a full-time schedule.

## Research areas to shortlist

Research five documented practices connected to:

- Seasonal food preservation or preparation.
- The first regular use of household warmth or light.
- A substantial fall meal or drink.
- Clothing, bedding, or household preparation for colder weather.
- A seasonal observance or practice connected to the harvest or the return of darkness.

For each practice, record what is documented, what is uncertain, what it practically provided, and whether a small modern version is safe and useful.

## Prototype structure after research

1. Opening: noticing the season has changed.
2. The old-world problem: preparing for colder days and longer nights.
3. Three practices worth carrying forward.
4. Five-minute, ten-minute, and weekend versions.
5. Evidence notes and safety boundaries.
6. A record page for what the reader tried.
7. Closing: prepare without trying to recreate the past.

## What this prototype tests

- Whether fall is a recognizable modern need.
- Whether the historical material feels useful rather than decorative.
- Whether the subject remains distinct from Kitchen & Kettle.
- Which practice deserves a longer follow-up or Lore Paper.

## Immediate next step

Research and shortlist five fall practices before choosing the three that belong in the first short editorial piece.
