# Visual Direction Notes — Exploratory Only

The uploaded image is an idea board, not a final identity.

## Useful signals

- Deep forest green, moss, umber, ochre, parchment, and charcoal
- Tactile materials: linen, rough paper, wood, stone, amber glass, dried botanicals
- Editorial serif typography with restrained botanical linework
- Warm, intimate still-life photography
- A world that feels studied, handmade, seasonal, and practical
- Old knowledge interpreted for modern life

## What to borrow carefully

- The earthy palette
- The archival and botanical feeling
- The combination of household practice, nature, food, and spiritual curiosity
- The contrast between useful work and quiet mystery

## What is not decided

- The name "Rooted in the Old Ways"
- The phrase "The Medicine Woman"
- Runes, knotwork, compass symbols, or other specific marks
- A dark, candlelit visual style
- Any particular product packaging system
- Whether the brand is specifically for women or simply centered on women's historical knowledge

## Guardrails

Avoid letting the visual language become:

- Generic witchy herbalism
- A Viking merchandise aesthetic
- Faux-antique history
- Dark and inaccessible wellness branding
- A costume version of traditional life

Keep room for daylight, modern kitchens, ordinary homes, research notes, clean information design, and real contemporary practice.

## Working interpretation

The useful core is:

> Earthy, intelligent, handmade, tradition-informed living.

The image should influence the atmosphere, not dictate the business, product line, or public identity.
