# Wise Woman

The research-led old-world line from Kitchen & Kettle. It brings carefully examined historical and traditional knowledge into ordinary modern life.

This is a line inside Kitchen & Kettle, not a separate brand or business.

## Working principle

Learn old knowledge carefully. Separate evidence from reconstruction. Test what belongs in modern life without pretending the past was simpler or safer than it was.

## Start here

- `strategy/project-brief.md` — current shape of the line
- `strategy/decisions.md` — choices that keep the line coherent
- `ideas/idea-inbox.md` — loose ideas captured before they disappear
- `experiments/` — small creative tests
- `research/` — source-based notes
- `prototypes/` — rough product or content forms
- `assets/` — line-specific images and working visual material

## First experiment

Make a small batch of 3–5 pieces around one clear question. Do not build a separate brand, website, broad product line, or posting schedule until the making itself proves enjoyable.

## Shared infrastructure

Website, deployment, product production, shop materials, and automation stay in the parent `Kitchen&Kettle/` directories. This folder holds Wise Woman's thinking and line-specific work.
