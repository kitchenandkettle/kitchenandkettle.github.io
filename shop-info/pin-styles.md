# Pinterest Pin Styles — Kitchen & Kettle

Quick reference so you don't forget what to tell Kit.

## Dark Overlay Pin (the good one)

Full-bleed photo + dark gradient overlay + centered text. This is the one that works.

### What to say

> "Make me a dark overlay pin for the \_\_\_ guide"

### Specs

- **Size:** 1000×1500 (2:3)
- **Background:** full-bleed photo, centered/covered
- **Overlay:** dark gradient — 45% opacity at top, 70% at bottom
- **Text color:** Paper (#F5F0E8)
- **Accent color:** warm gold/clay (#D4C4A8) for label, hook, bullets, divider, brand bar
- **Fonts:** Crimson Pro (titles/hook) + Work Sans (label/brand bar)
- **Text shadow:** yes — 2px blur on titles, 1px on hook

### Layout (top to bottom, centered)

1. **Label** — 24px Work Sans, uppercase (e.g. "PRINTABLE GUIDE", "PRINTABLE CARDS")
2. **Title** — 100px Crimson Pro, 2-3 lines max
3. **Hook** — 42px Crimson Pro italic in accent color, one line
4. **Divider** — 60px accent line
5. **Features** — 4 bullet points, 34px Crimson Pro in accent color
6. **Brand bar** — 22px Work Sans "KITCHEN & KETTLE", bottom-50px

### Example

Egg Handling Cards pin at `digital-products/egg-handling-card/pin.png`:

- Photo: annie-spratt eggs on rustic surface
- Label: "PRINTABLE CARDS"
- Title: "Farm Fresh / Egg Handling / Cards"
- Hook: "Wash, store, float test — made simple."
- Features: Six cards per sheet · Tuck into every carton · No guesswork, just warm water · Printable PDF · 2 pages

### Photo requirements

- Warm tones, natural light, rustic feel
- Subject related to the product
- Some empty space or even texture for text overlay
- Dark enough for light text to read (the overlay helps, but don't start with a bright white photo)
