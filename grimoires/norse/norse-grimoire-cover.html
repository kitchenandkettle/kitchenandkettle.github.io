A Grimoire of the Old Religions · Vol. I

A working journal of worlds, gods, oath & craft.

Short passages open each section. Prompts and lined pages do the rest. Write in it. Date your entries. Cross things out.

  • The Nine Realms, kept the northern way
  • Gods, neighbors, heroes, and your own oath
  • Runes, staves, plants, and liminal places
  • Charms, ancestors, and oath-keeping

Honest about sources. Nothing here claims a closed lineage. Take what fits a full modern life. Leave the rest.
